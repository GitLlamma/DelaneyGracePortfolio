What to Expect

Healthy Technique First

Every lesson begins with guided warm-ups and exercises designed to build breath support, resonance, and range — without strain. Your voice health is always the top priority.

Repertoire You Love

You will work on real music: art songs, arias, musical theater solos, and folk pieces chosen to match your level and goals. Learning songs you enjoy keeps practice meaningful.

Language & Diction

Delaney is fluent in Spanish and teaches accurate diction in Italian, German, French, and Latin — essential for classical and art song repertoire.

Performance Preparation

Students preparing for auditions, recitals, or competitions will receive focused coaching on stage presence, memorization strategies, and stylistic interpretation.

Studio Policies

  • Cancellations: Please provide at least 24 hours' notice to cancel or reschedule a lesson. Late cancellations may be charged at full rate.
  • Location: Lessons are held in-home in Mapleton, UT. Online lessons via Zoom are also available upon request.
  • Age: Students ages 7 and up are welcome. Younger students are accepted on a case-by-case basis.
  • Materials: Sheet music and warm-up materials are provided. Students are encouraged to bring a practice journal.
  • Payment: Payment is due at or before each lesson. Monthly packages are billed at the start of the month.
