Biography

Delaney Grace Johns-Esplin

Delaney is a classically trained singer and voice teacher with a B.A. in Vocal Performance from Utah Valley University. She has experience in a variety of styles including opera, folk song, art song, and musical theater, giving her a strong foundation in vocal technique and performance styles.

She has performed in choral, musical theater, and opera productions throughout Utah County, including roles with UVU's Opera program and Hale Center Theater Orem. Her studies under Dr. Anthony Buck and Dr. Demaree Brown have instilled in her a deep respect for music, a healthy dose of humor, and a commitment to honoring the unique voice of every singer.

With over two years of private teaching experience, Delaney brings warmth and expertise to every lesson. She firmly believes everyone deserves a chance to discover their own voice and share their music with others in a way that builds confidence and connection.
