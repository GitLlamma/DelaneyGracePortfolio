Biography

Delaney Grace Johns-Esplin

Delaney is a vocalist and educator with a B.A. in Vocal Performance from Utah Valley University. Her training spans classical, folk song, art song, and musical theater, giving her both the technical foundation and the stylistic range to guide students across genres.

She has performed in musical theater and opera productions throughout Utah, including roles with Heritage Center Theater Orem (HCTO) and UVU's opera program. Her studies under Kahli Dalbow, Dr. Anthony Buck, and Dr. Demaree Brown have shaped a teaching philosophy grounded in healthy technique, expressive freedom, and genuine encouragement.

With over two years of private teaching experience in the community, Delaney brings warmth and expertise to every lesson. She believes every voice is unique and every student deserves instruction that meets them where they are.
