What to Expect

Healthy Technique First

Every lesson starts with guided warm-ups designed to strengthen breath support, improve resonance, and increase range. Healthy singing is always the top priority.

Repertoire You Love

I will work with you to choose music that meets your level and goals, while giving you opportunities to grow. Learning songs you enjoy makes practice more meaningful.

Performance Preparation

Whether you're preparing for an audition, recital, or competition, I will work with you on stage presence, memorization, and engaging storytelling. Music is most meaningful when it is shared.

Studio Policies

  • Cancellations: Please provide at least 24 hours' notice to cancel or reschedule a lesson. Late cancellations may be charged at full rate.
  • Age: Students ages 12 and up are welcome. Younger students are accepted on a case-by-case basis.
  • Recording Lessons: Students are expected to record their lessons for review and practice during the week.
  • Payment: Payment is due at or before each lesson. Payments must be made using cash, check, or Venmo.
